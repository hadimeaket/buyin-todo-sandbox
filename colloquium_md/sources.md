# Sources & citation mapping (slides)

## Thesis core contribution (2 sentences)

This thesis contributes a documented, auditable evaluation logic for interactive AI-assisted software development in a controlled brownfield sandbox by separating outcome evidence into the pipeline stages Attempted, Implemented_static, and Execution observed (plus the derived evidence level Implemented\*). It operationalizes these stages via versioned code, execution, chat, and survey artefacts and triangulates them to avoid single-metric interpretations of “progress” under timeboxed brownfield conditions.
